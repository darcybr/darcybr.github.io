Make Text With Your Own Cool Font in TinkerCAD! 🎨

Want your name or a word to look totally different — spooky, bubbly, blocky, whatever you like? You can bring ANY font into TinkerCAD and turn it into a 3D shape. Here's how, in 3 easy parts.

1

Find & Download a Font

  1. Go to dafont.com on your computer.
  2. Type something into the search bar to find a font style you like — try "bubble," "spooky," or "block."
  3. Click on a font's name to see a bigger preview.
  4. Click the green Download button.
  5. The font will download as a .zip file into your Downloads folder.
Tip: Pick a font that isn't too thin or too fancy — simple, bold shapes work best for 3D printing!
2

Unzip It & Convert to SVG

  1. Find the .zip file you just downloaded (check your Downloads folder).
  2. Right-click the .zip file and choose Extract All (Windows) or double-click it (Mac) to unzip it.
  3. Open the unzipped folder. Look for a file that ends in .ttf or .otf — that's your font file!
  4. Go to cloudconvert.com on your computer.
  5. Click Select File and choose your .ttf or .otf font file.
  6. Change the "Convert to" format to SVG.
  7. Click Convert, then click Download to save your new SVG file. Make sure it saves to your Downloads folder.
Tip: The file name matters — remember what you named your SVG so you can find it in the next step!
3

Bring It Into TinkerCAD

  1. Go to tinkercad.com and open your design.
  2. On the right side of the screen, click Shape Generators.
  3. Scroll down to the Text category and find Custom Font Text.
  4. Drag Custom Font Text onto your workplane.
  5. Click on the shape, then find the Add SVG button in its properties panel.
  6. Upload the SVG font file you saved in Section 2.
  7. Type your text into the text box — it will magically appear in your new font!
  8. Resize and move it just like any other shape.
Tip: If your text looks strange or doesn't show up, double check you uploaded the .svg file (not the .ttf) in this step.
