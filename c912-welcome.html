Welcome to Computers 9–12

This course introduces students to a wide range of digital tools, programming environments, and design platforms. Students work with TinkerCAD, Fusion 360, Blender, Adobe Creative Cloud, and the Godot game engine to develop skills in both creative and technical applications.

Topics include 3D modeling, CAD, electronic circuits, Micro:bit projects, Arduino systems, and game design with GDScript in Godot. These areas provide opportunities to combine problem solving, creativity, and practical application.

Programming is an essential component of the course. Students use MakeCode, Visual Studio Code, and Visual Studio Community to learn and apply programming languages including Python, JavaScript, C#, and GDScript.

By the end of the course, students gain experience in designing, coding, and developing projects that demonstrate a broad understanding of modern computing.
