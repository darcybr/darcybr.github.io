Make Your Own Name Tag in TinkerCAD! 🏷️

Follow these steps to build a flat name tag with your name on it — perfect for your bag, your desk, or a gift!

1

Make the Base Shape

  1. Open tinkercad.com and start a new design.
  2. On the right side, find the Basic Shapes panel and drag a Box onto the workplane.
  3. Click on the box to select it. Small white boxes (handles) will appear on its corners and edges — these let you resize it.
  4. Look for the shape's dimensions. You can usually click directly on the shape's numbers, or use the side/corner handles to type exact sizes.
  5. Set the size to 60 x 60 x 2 mm (a square tag) — or if you'd rather have a longer rectangle name tag, use 60 x 30 x 2 mm instead.
  6. This flat box is your name tag's base.
Tip: The first number is usually length, the second is width, and the third is height. A height of 2mm keeps your tag thin and light.
2

Add Your Name With Text

  1. In the shapes panel, find the Text tool (it looks like the letter "A") and drag it onto your base, right on top.
  2. Click the text and type your name (or a nickname) into the text box that appears.
  3. Use the handles to resize the text so it fits nicely on your base — not too big, not too tiny.
  4. Move the text so it sits centered on top of the base. Use the little black arrow (the up/down handle) to raise the text slightly so it's sitting on top of the box, not sunk inside it.
  5. When it looks right, select both the base and the text (click one, then hold Shift and click the other), and click Group to combine them into one shape.
Tip: Want your name in a totally different style? Check out the Custom Font page linked below!
✨ Added Challenge
3

Cut a Star or Triangle With the Hole Tool

In TinkerCAD, every shape can be either a solid (something you can see and touch) or a hole (something that cuts through other shapes, like a cookie cutter). This is called negative space — using an empty shape to remove material instead of add it.

  1. Drag a Star or Polygon (triangle) shape from the Basic Shapes panel onto your workplane, near your name tag.
  2. With the star or triangle selected, click the "Solid" button in its properties box and change it to "Hole". It will turn see-through with red stripes — that means it's ready to cut.
  3. Resize the hole shape so it's small enough to fit somewhere on your name tag (maybe in a corner, or between letters).
  4. Move the hole shape so it overlaps your base. Make sure it's tall enough to go all the way through the base (raise or stretch it if needed) — if it doesn't fully poke through both sides, it won't cut all the way.
  5. Select both your name tag and the hole shape, then click Group. The star or triangle will disappear, leaving a cutout shape in your name tag!
Tip: You can add more than one hole shape before grouping — try cutting out a few stars for a fun pattern!
