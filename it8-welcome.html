Welcome to IT 8

This short course introduces students to information technology through practical, hands-on activities.

Students work with MakeCode block coding to develop programming fundamentals, then extend their skills with Python. The course also explores sustainable IT design, encouraging students to consider the environmental impact of technology.

In addition, students practice 3D modeling, gaining experience with tools and techniques used in digital design.

By the end of the course, students will have a foundation in programming, design thinking, and responsible technology use.
