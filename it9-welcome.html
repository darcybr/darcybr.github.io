Welcome to IT 9 and Careers

This combined course connects information technology with career education, helping students build both technical and personal skills for the future.

Students develop essential life skills, practice planning a graduation program, and learn about workplace safety and rights. The course also emphasizes digital citizenship and safety online, helping students understand how to use technology responsibly.

Building on concepts from IT 8, students apply programming and design skills with an emphasis on the Internet of Things and smart home technology. These applications highlight how digital systems connect to the physical world.

By the end of the course, students will have experience in both career planning and modern technology, with a focus on practical, real-world applications.
